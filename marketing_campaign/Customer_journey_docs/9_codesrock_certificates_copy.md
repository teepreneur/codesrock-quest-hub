# Codesrock Certificate Copy & Wording Guide

This document contains the official text, layout guidelines, and gamified branding copy for all 5 official Codesrock certificates. Designed for early-grade educators and young learners, each certificate balances gamified motivation (Rocky the Logic Star, Quest Ranks, Badges, XP) with formal academic prestige.

> **Official Certificate Signature Policy:**  
> Every official Codesrock certificate features exactly two official sign-offs:
> 1. **Codesrock Representative (School Supervisor)** — The supervising Codesrock officer directly overseeing implementation, training, and quality delivery at the school.
> 2. **Codesrock Director** — Executive director approving overall curriculum completion and institutional certification.

---

## 1. Teacher Certificate: Course Completion
**Purpose:** Awarded to teachers who successfully complete the Codesrock Educator Onboarding & Level 1 Pedagogy Training Course.

* **Certificate Title:** CERTIFICATE OF COMPLETION

* **Award Citation:**  
  *This is to certify that*  
  # **[ TEACHER FULL NAME ]**
